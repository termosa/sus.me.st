# Built-in night music

All tracks are dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0) by their authors and were downloaded
from OpenGameArt. Each was trimmed of silence, set to −14 LUFS and encoded as 96 kbps MP3.
Tracks that faded out at the end were cut before the fade and crossfaded (3 s) into their
own opening, so they loop without a dip.
