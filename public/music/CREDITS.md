# Built-in night music

All three tracks are dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0) by their authors and were
downloaded from OpenGameArt. They were re-encoded to 128 kbps MP3 and loudness-matched.

| File | Source | Author | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| rain.mp3 | [Rain (loopable)](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable) | Ylmir | The four loops joined with 3 s crossfades |
| calm.mp3 | [Heavenly Loop](https://opengameart.org/content/heavenly-loop) | isaiah658 | Repeated 4 times, from Ogg Vorbis |
| tension.mp3 | [Paranoid Truth](https://opengameart.org/content/paranoid-truth) | Tsorthan Grove | Cover art removed |
